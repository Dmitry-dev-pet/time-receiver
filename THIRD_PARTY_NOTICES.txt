Shortwave Radio Audio Archive interval signals
https://shortwavearchive.com/

The radio transition catalogue links to six recordings hosted by the source:
Radio Mayak, Voice of America, BBC World Service (two variants),
Deutsche Welle and Radio Berlin International. The recordings are not copied
into this repository. Their source pages identify them as licensed under the
Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 3.0 Unported License:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/

The receiver labels these recordings PERIOD or EQUIVALENT. It does not claim
that they were broadcast at the selected boundary or on the selected date.

Wikimedia Commons radio callsigns
https://commons.wikimedia.org/

The radio transition catalogue links to two public-domain recordings hosted by
Wikimedia Commons. The files are not copied into this repository:

All-Union Radio, "Wide Is My Motherland" interval signal (1941)
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wide_Is_My_Motherland_in_1941.ogg

Radio Liberty, "Anthem of Free Russia" callsign (published 2020)
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%D0%9F%D0%BE%D0%B7%D1%8B%D0%B2%D0%BD%D1%8B%D0%B5_%D0%A0%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BE_%D0%A1%D0%B2%D0%BE%D0%B1%D0%BE%D0%B4%D0%B0.ogg

For historical dates the receiver labels both only PERIOD or EQUIVALENT. The
modern Radio Liberty rendering is never presented as a recording from the
selected Soviet-era date.
